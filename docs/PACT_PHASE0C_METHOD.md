# Phase-0C method and inference boundary

Phase-0B is frozen evidence. Its saved analysis was reproduced without rewriting it: 15/15 complete design×seed pairs, 90 qualified routes, 2 practically meaningful threshold hits, zero designs with 4/5 replicated hits, and `PACT_PHASE0B_CONFLICT_NOT_REPLICATED_PHASE1_NO_GO`. The SHA256 list for 4,127 relevant files is `artifacts/manifests/phase0c/phase0b_pre_edit.sha256`. The pre-edit `main` HEAD was `06fe31583b9822368cebe5b98c0e16766969af7f`; Phase-0B content was untracked and is not staged into Phase-0C commits.

The candidate experiment uses the three previously qualified designs: s5378 (179 scan FFs, 117 FAN patterns, 96.04% stuck-at coverage), s9234 (211, 156, 94.14%), and s15850 (534, 133, 94.62%). The [candidate audit](../artifacts/manifests/phase0c/benchmark_candidate_audit.json) records ORFS Ibex and JPEG source URLs, licenses, configs and hashes; neither has a qualified FAN full-scan PPI/FF mapping, so neither is admitted. Original ISCAS89 redistribution rights remain unresolved. Physical seeds 11/13/17/19/23 are conditional perturb-and-legalize replicas around one source placement. Every variant starts from the same seed-specific ODB. Architecture proposal seed 101 and descriptive bootstrap seed 0 are separate.

The six-family Cartesian route matrix has `3 × 5 × 4 × 6 = 360` variants. The actual verified OpenROAD-native B1 order is rerouted at K=1 for each design×seed under the same Phase-0C port policy, giving **375 final routes**. The three K=8 B0 route pilots took 76, 116 and 168 seconds on s5378, s9234 and s15850. Multiplying these representative times by 125 routes/design gives roughly 12.5 hours before variability, setup, retries and analysis. The frozen contract specifies a **20-hour absolute campaign cap**, a 900-second per-run route cap, and a 512-MiB workspace free-space floor. Incomplete work remains visible.

`B0` splits supplied order, `P` uses balanced x partitions and nearest-neighbor ordering, `A` uses bit-profile mismatch without coordinates, `J50` uses a preselected 50/50 normalized geometric/mismatch edge cost, `T` uses squared normalized edge distance as a long-edge risk proxy, and `R` shuffles a fixed FF list with architecture seed 101. Additional `J25/J75` are implemented but not planned for physical routing. `T` is only a timing-risk heuristic; actual setup/hold effects require routed timing. The B1 reference is the actual OpenROAD Phase-0B native `K=1` order; no split B1 is passed off as native.

Every generated architecture preserves the FF bijection and reconstructs every frozen FAN PPI target using [tail-first parallel loading](PACT_PHASE0C_MULTICHAIN_SEMANTICS.md). All K chains shift for Lmax cycles; shorter chains receive leading zero inputs. The independent verifier checks the final FF state and scan-out path for every pattern. Functional FF D, clock and reset connections and instance placement are invariant in the ODB rewrite. A post-route verifier follows each SI/SO path through inserted transparent buffers and checks fixed port coordinates.

The primary physical proxy is **port-aware FF-origin scan HPWL**: internal FF-to-FF Manhattan links plus all 2K SI/SO port-to-FF Manhattan links. It is not routed wirelength. The Nangate45 metal5 policy selects the nearest free `140+560n` DBU Y track, with a 280-DBU pin and at least 1,120 DBU clearance from existing edge pins. The original `test_so` port is relocated by the same architecture-independent rule for every variant. The [port policy](../src/pact/physical/phase0c_port_policy.py), [metric implementation](../src/pact/physical/phase0c_scan_geometry.py), and routed-port verifier agree. An earlier pre-freeze pilot proxy omitted port links; its original metrics were preserved and corrected. For s5378/s11/B0/K8, internal FF-edge HPWL is 2,063.79 µm and port links add 993.81 µm.

Detailed-route DRC, full-netlist route wirelength/vias, global-route setup/hold WNS/TNS and DRV counts are tool outputs. The [saved-log parser](../src/pact/physical/phase0c_congestion.py) extracts **initial global-route** per-layer resource, demand, usage and overflow; it is not detailed-route or post-repair congestion. Summed routed lengths over all verified SI-to-SO transparent-buffer nets are a **full-scan-path upper bound**, because mixed functional branches can inflate them. Exact scan-only routed length is null unless all path nets are proven exclusive; none of the pilots qualifies. Negative WNS is preserved. The [power-proxy method](PACT_PHASE0C_POWER_PROXY.md) separates exact no-capture logical shift toggles from H8/H16/H32 and direct-sink-weighted H_eff proxies. Shift-mode power and test-mode IR drop remain unqualified and null.

The [frozen C1–C9 thresholds](../config/phase0c_analysis_contract.json) require all 375 deterministic, verified, hash-provenanced proxy rows (C1); at least 90% qualified final routes, 80% complete method groups and all three designs (C2); a paired ≥5% H_eff gain and ≥10% port-aware physical-proxy penalty (C3); hits in at least 4/5 seeds on one design (C4) and two designs (C5). C6 asks whether **either one simple method or their fixed deterministic portfolio** 2%-covers the primary Pareto set in at least 80% of complete groups. C7 tests at least 20 fixed local swaps around each physically qualified P/K2 parent, with at least 20% of each port-aware HPWL-proxy delta sign across at least four seeds on two designs. Children are structurally verified but not called routed. C8 counts ordered labelled nonempty chain assignments as `N! × binomial(N−1,K−1)` and requires log10 count ≥100. C9 passes only when C1–C8 all pass. The thresholds and exact analysis code are committed before final outcomes are generated.

The small qualification used s5378, seed 11. K=1 B0 exactly replays Phase-0B's 1,954,773 logical toggles and H8=4.15556. All 25 physical variants across K=1/2/4/8, six methods and native K=1 B1 have zero detailed-route DRC and pass post-route FF/port/SI/SO checks; separate K=8 B0 pilots on s9234 and s15850 also pass. Early off-grid and overlapping-port DRC failures and timeouts remain archived. All 25 pilot ODB snapshots were losslessly gzip-compressed with raw and archive hashes. These are **pre-freeze qualification results**, not final campaign routes. The [final campaign driver](../scripts/phase0c_campaign_run.py) gives each post-freeze variant a freeze-commit prefix, preserves the original absolute deadline on restart, and resumes only hash-valid outputs. The fixed intervention screen has its own [bounded driver](../scripts/phase0c_run_intervention_screen.py). The primary experimental unit remains design×physical seed; K and architecture variants within it are paired observations, not independent design samples. Median/IQR, seed-level replication, heuristic regret and Pareto membership are descriptive without population claims from five conditional seeds.
